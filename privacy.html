Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

What this covers

This policy describes the Google Form Auto Filler Chrome extension and the public website at formautofiller.com. The current release does not provide user accounts, paid plans, or a server database for saved form answers.

Extension data

The extension stores saved question-and-answer pairs and random-fill lists in Chrome storage so they can be reused. Unfinished drafts are stored locally in the browser. The current extension does not upload those saved answers to the Google Form Auto Filler website.

Website analytics

If Vercel Web Analytics is enabled, the website may receive aggregated usage information such as page views, referring pages, browser and device categories, and approximate location. This is used to understand whether the website is useful and to improve it. The website does not send your extension answer data to analytics.

Third-party services

The site links to the Chrome Web Store and Google Forms. Those services have their own privacy policies and may process information according to their terms. The extension also depends on Chrome storage and Google Forms pages when you choose to use it.

Changes and questions

This policy may be updated when the extension or website changes. For privacy questions or support, use the support page or the Chrome Web Store listing.
